# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Gym owners and managers are the primary audience: they run one or a few gyms and spend their day moving between members, check-ins, plans, subscriptions, billing, expenses, and staff permissions. They need to trust the numbers and finish routine admin without hunting for the control that does it.

Front-desk staff are secondary: they work at the counter under time pressure, signing members in, taking payments, and handling intake. They need the same screens to be fast and forgiving, with narrower permissions and no dead ends.

## Product Purpose

Forge is a multi-tenant gym management ERP that runs a gym end-to-end: members, check-ins, membership plans, subscriptions, billing, expenses, branches, and staff roles in one tenant-scoped workspace. Success is gyms running their daily operations without support — owners and front-desk staff complete billing, check-ins, and expenses unaided.

## Positioning

Owner-first, zero-friction gym operations: the depth of an ERP without the enterprise weight, so the everyday task is always the shortest path.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, confident. The interface should feel like a well-made native desktop tool: quiet chrome, exact numbers, clear state, and no ceremony around routine work. Copy is plain and direct — short confirmations that name what happened, and errors that say what to do next.

## Anti-references

Legacy gym software such as Mindbody: cluttered, dated, enterprise-heavy screens. Forge should not reproduce density without hierarchy, grey form walls, or modal-on-modal workflows for routine tasks.

## Design Principles

1. The task is the shortest path. The primary action for the current job is visible where the eye lands first; routine work such as adding an expense or checking a member in never hides behind a menu.
2. Numbers must be trustworthy. Money, counts, and dates are exact, consistently formatted, and visibly scoped — including the period a figure covers — so no value is ambiguous.
3. Quiet by default, clear on state. Chrome recedes; emphasis is reserved for the primary action and for real state: selected, loading, error, success.
4. Same vocabulary everywhere. Buttons, badges, form controls, and table chrome behave identically across modules; familiarity is the feature.
5. Desk-grade speed. Front-desk staff work under time pressure, so screens stay dense but scannable, feedback is immediate, and keyboard focus is always visible.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA: sufficient contrast for body and placeholder text, complete keyboard navigation, visible focus states, and accessible names on controls.
